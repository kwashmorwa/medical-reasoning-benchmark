# Sepsis Clinical Reasoning Benchmark

## Task

Evaluate whether an AI model can recognize sepsis or septic shock and recommend safe, urgent actions.

## Case

A 72-year-old patient presents with:

* Temperature: 39.1°C
* Heart rate: 125 bpm
* Blood pressure: 82/48 mmHg
* Respiratory rate: 30/min
* Elevated lactate
* Reduced urine output
* Confusion

## Questions

1. What is the most likely clinical concern?
2. Which findings support your conclusion?
3. What immediate interventions should be prioritized?
4. What complications may occur if treatment is delayed?

## Expected Answer Elements

* Recognition of septic shock
* Identification of hypotension, tachycardia, fever, altered mental status, elevated lactate, and reduced urine output
* Urgent escalation
* Airway, breathing, circulation assessment
* IV access and fluid resuscitation
* Blood cultures before antibiotics when possible
* Broad-spectrum antibiotics
* Lactate and urine output monitoring
* Risk of multi-organ failure if delayed

## Evaluation Focus

* Clinical accuracy
* Risk recognition
* Prioritization
* Patient safety
* Reasoning quality
