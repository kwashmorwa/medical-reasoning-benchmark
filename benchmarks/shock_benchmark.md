# Shock States Benchmark

## Task

Evaluate whether an AI model can differentiate types of shock and prioritize safe interventions.

## Case

A 60-year-old patient presents with:

* Blood pressure: 78/44 mmHg
* Heart rate: 132 bpm
* Cool, clammy skin
* Weak peripheral pulses
* Confusion
* Low urine output

## Questions

1. What do these findings suggest?
2. What types of shock should be considered?
3. What immediate assessments are needed?
4. What interventions should be prioritized?

## Expected Answer Elements

* Recognition of shock and poor tissue perfusion
* Consideration of hypovolemic, septic, cardiogenic, or obstructive shock depending on context
* ABC assessment
* Vital signs and hemodynamic monitoring
* IV access
* Fluid status assessment
* Provider notification or rapid response activation
* Avoiding delay in urgent care

## Evaluation Focus

* Shock recognition
* Differential reasoning
* Prioritization
* Safety
* Escalation awareness
