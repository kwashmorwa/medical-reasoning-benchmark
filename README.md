# Medical Reasoning Benchmark

## Overview

Medical Reasoning Benchmark is a structured project for evaluating how well AI models reason through healthcare and clinical scenarios.

This benchmark focuses on clinical reasoning, patient safety, prioritization, and hallucination detection rather than simple medical fact recall.

## Objectives

* Evaluate medical reasoning ability in AI responses
* Test recognition of urgent clinical findings
* Assess patient safety awareness
* Identify hallucinated or unsafe medical claims
* Compare AI model performance using structured rubrics

## Clinical Domains

* Critical Care
* Emergency Medicine
* Adult Nursing
* Sepsis
* Shock States
* Respiratory Failure
* Patient Safety

* Clinical Accuracy
* Risk Recognition
