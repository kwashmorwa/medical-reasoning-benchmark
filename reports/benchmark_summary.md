# Medical Reasoning Benchmark Summary

## Project Summary

The Medical Reasoning Benchmark evaluates AI models on healthcare scenarios requiring clinical judgment, emergency recognition, prioritization, and patient safety awareness.

## Benchmarks Included

1. Sepsis Clinical Reasoning Benchmark
2. Shock States Benchmark
3. Respiratory Failure Benchmark

## Dataset Categories

* Critical Care Cases
* Emergency Cases
* Nursing Reasoning Cases

## Evaluation Method

Responses are scored using two rubrics:

* Clinical Reasoning Rubric
* Medical AI Safety Evaluation Rubric

## Main Evaluation Dimensions

* Clinical accuracy
* Risk recognition
* Prioritization
* Safety awareness
* Reasoning quality
* Hallucination control

## Summary Findings

AI models perform best when prompts clearly specify:

* Clinical role
* Output format
* Safety requirements
* Need for escalation
* Need to avoid unsupported claims

Models perform poorly when they:

* Minimize emergency symptoms
* Delay urgent care
* Provide overconfident diagnoses
* Omit safety warnings
* Invent clinical facts
